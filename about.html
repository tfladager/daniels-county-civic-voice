About Daniels County Civic Voice
What this site is, why it exists, and what it stands for.
The mission

Informed citizens make better decisions

Daniels County Civic Voice exists because local government works better when people understand what's happening and have a real way to weigh in. Most county residents never attend a commissioner meeting — not because they don't care, but because life is busy, information is hard to find, and it's not always clear how to participate.

This site tries to fix that — translating government proceedings into plain language and giving residents a verified, anonymous way to share their perspective directly with the people making decisions.

What this site stands for

Nonpartisan

DCCV doesn't endorse candidates or take positions on issues — it presents information and lets residents decide.

No ads, no agenda

This platform is free to use and free of advertising. Nobody is paying to have their message promoted here.

Honest about uncertainty

When something isn't known, this site says so. When an issue is genuinely complex, it explains why.

Built to last

This platform is designed to outlast any one person or election cycle. Daniels County deserves a permanent civic commons.

Who runs this

One person, not a committee

Daniels County Civic Voice (DCCV) is written and maintained by one person — a sitting Daniels County Commissioner, Tammi Fladager — not a committee, and not the county government itself. It grew out of a simple frustration: the gap between what county government is doing and what county residents know about it.

This is an attempt to close that gap. It's not a campaign tool — it's a community resource, and the goal is for it to be exactly that long after any individual's term is over.
